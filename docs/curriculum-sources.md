## Transcription and validation

All four scanned pages were reviewed visually. BSIT contains 49 scheduled subject entries totaling 146 units; BSCS contains 51 totaling 146 units. Year, semester, Summer, code, description, units and printed prerequisites are preserved in curriculum/plp-2022.js. Both program descriptions for COMP 106 are retained, including the printed Application / Applications wording. GE elective 1–3 remain the coded slots printed in the source; the unnumbered elective options are not counted as additional subjects or assigned invented codes. Lecture/laboratory contact hours are not enrollment units and are not imported as additional credits.

Summer is stored as curriculum semester 3 and displayed as Summer. The current enrollment calendar supports Semesters 1 and 2; Summer enrollment is not enabled by importing the catalog.

Printed year-standing prerequisites use the supplied year-level identifier tables: BSIT 3rd year requires at least 81 passed curriculum units; BSCS 2nd year at least 40, and 4th year at least 118. The student must also have the required recorded year level. Failed and Not taken records earn no prerequisite credit. Practice EX subjects do not contribute to the supplied curriculum’s standing totals.

## Existing records

Import is transactional and repeatable. Existing students, passwords, plans and allocations are preserved. Historical sample section titles are retained. A reused code with an unrelated historical title is not treated as an available section of the supplied curriculum. New sections snapshot their selected curriculum title. Existing academic credits with an incompatible code/title or units must be reviewed before import; they are never silently remapped.

The evaluator stores a current result per subject, not a full transcript of attempts. Applying multiple curriculum versions to different cohorts remains a separate feature. The source label identifies these entries without changing students’ intake or enrollment term.
