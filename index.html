How enrollment works

Four steps. School guidance along the way.

  1. Register your details

    Register your details and program. Keep the permanent student ID you receive; it stays with you across terms.

  2. School review

    The Registrar reviews your record and assigns regular subjects. The Department Head handles special subject assignments.

  3. Accept or decline

    Sign in to review the complete schedule prepared for you. Accept or decline your enrollment offer.

  4. Registrar confirmation

    After Registrar confirmation, find your confirmed subjects and timetable in your student account.

Enrollment questions

Choose a question to see the answer.

University Registrar

Do I need an account to register?

No. Submit your student details without an account. After registration, set up your first password when you are ready to review schedule offers.

Can I choose my own subjects or sections?

The Registrar assigns regular subjects and prepares schedules. The Department Head assigns subjects for special cases. Accept or decline the complete offer when it is ready.

I already have a student ID. Should I register again?

Keep your existing ID. Check your registration or sign in to continue. Your student identity stays the same across terms.

How do I correct my registration details?

Ask the Registrar to review your record. Staff verify corrections before updating your information. Your permanent student ID does not change.
